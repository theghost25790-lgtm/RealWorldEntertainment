# Advertising Principles

Project 2088 is intended to remain free to players and may be funded through selective, world-integrated advertising and sponsorship.

## Player promise

- Advertising should belong naturally inside Neo-Victoria.
- Advertising must not obstruct the player's view or movement.
- No forced countdown should hold gameplay hostage.
- Sponsors do not purchase creative control over the story.
- Real advertising should remain balanced with fictional commerce, public information, propaganda and environmental storytelling.
- Campaigns must be appropriate for the audience and the project's creative direction.

## Persistent narrative campaigns

A campaign may develop across multiple builds:

1. Announcement or teaser
2. Preparation and environmental change
3. Playable event or launch
4. Consequences and public reaction
5. Historical references in later builds

This allows advertising to contribute to the evolving world while preserving each campaign's place in the playable build archive.

## Measurement

Early campaigns may be supported by release downloads, page traffic and documented placements. Any future in-game analytics must be introduced transparently with appropriate privacy controls.
