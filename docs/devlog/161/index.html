RWE ARCHIVE // PROJECT 2088 // DEVELOPMENT RECORD

THE WORLD
REMEMBERS

Project 2088 formalises the layer that lets Albion observe, destabilise, recover and remember — while building directly on the AI systems already in place.

RWE-P2088-DEV-16121 SEP 2026PUBLISHED

Development record

The next world-simulation layer is being designed around World Cells: explorable areas with their own population, activity, stability and history.

The goal is not to script every encounter. The world decides who is present and what pressures exist; the existing perception, relationship and combat systems decide what happens when those actors meet.

Cell Health

Cell Health measures civic resilience: whether ordinary life can keep functioning.

Infrastructure, supply, connectivity, security confidence, civic continuity and social cohesion can all contribute. Health remains deliberately separate from faction control. A Free City can be healthy. A Construct district can be unhealthy.

Low health does not automatically create combat. It first changes the texture of the place: fewer deliveries, closed shops, queues, nervous civilians, rumours, black markets, heavier patrols and reduced travel.

Conflict Pressure

Conflict Pressure tracks the forces acting against a cell: faction pressure, scarcity, recent violence, isolation, player-caused disruption and spillover from neighbouring cells.

Low Cell Health creates vulnerability. Pressure decides whether that vulnerability becomes conflict.

When pressure rises against a fragile cell, the World Activity Director increasingly weights raids, territorial incidents and faction encounters. The AI is then free to resolve those meetings through its normal perception and relationship logic.

Built on existing infrastructure

The new layer extends rather than replaces the current systems.

The world remembers

Observation is being treated separately from reporting. Civilians, workers, Blanks, traders and security can notice unusual behaviour without immediately becoming hostile.

Significant observations can become witness memory, evidence, rumour or faction knowledge. Reputation therefore follows known actions, not omniscient game-state changes.

The player remains free to walk toward an event, walk away, intervene, observe or ignore it. Given the chance, the world can remember what they chose.

Albion doctrine

Albion remains a living England rather than a dead wasteland. Destruction is localised. Repair is more common than ruin. Most places remain inhabited and useful.

That makes abnormal emptiness meaningful. A recently raided property can feel dangerous because the street around it is still alive — and because nearby people may quietly watch who goes inside.

Nobody stops you. Everybody sees you.

Recovery is intended to be as systemic as collapse. Restored transport, water, trade, security confidence or community cohesion can raise Cell Health and change what the player finds on a later visit.

First proving ground

FarmersField is the natural first test. Rail disruption, supply pressure, Scav activity and Construct security can all change its population profile without rebuilding the physical map.

The same roads and buildings can therefore support quiet normality, shortages, observation, recovery or spontaneous conflict depending on current state.

Next priorities

Define the first Cell Health data structure, Conflict Pressure inputs, Activity Director weighting rules and the minimum Witness Memory event format. Expose all four to QA so a tester can see not only what happened, but why the cell was predisposed toward it.
