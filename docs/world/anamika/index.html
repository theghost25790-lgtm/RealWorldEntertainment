ANAMIKA

Human advancement

Anamika's core belief is that humanity can be perfected. Its institutional voice is clinical, scientific and optimistic rather than openly cruel.

Its work spans pharmaceuticals, medical research, augmentation and human experimentation, often presented internally as advancement rather than violation.

Containment work

Project 2088's containment material places Anamika alongside Construct in high-security biohazard research. Construct controls infrastructure and containment; Anamika studies what has been contained.

Doctrine

Humanity can be perfected.

The danger is not that Anamika believes humanity has no value. It believes humanity is valuable enough to redesign.
