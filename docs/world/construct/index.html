CONSTRUCT

The systems that keep Britana alive did not stop when governments failed. Construct made continuity into doctrine.

RWE-P2088-FACTION-CONSTRUCTPUBLISHED

Order as survival

Construct's institutional worldview is simple: disorder is not merely untidiness or disobedience. Disorder is what happens when power, water, food, transport and distribution fail.

Its culture was shaped by generations that remember failed supply chains, hunger, disease and war. That history is why Construct speaks about control as protection rather than conquest.

Infrastructure authority

Construct is associated with infrastructure, security, stability, Hive administration, logistics, resource processing and the systems that keep communities supplied.

Its public voice is calm, reassuring and bureaucratic. Even when force escalates, the organisation presents intervention as necessary to prevent a larger human cost.

Doctrine

“Construct provides.”

In Project 2088 that line can sound comforting or threatening depending on where the player stands.
