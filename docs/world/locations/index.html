DIAGRAM 06 // CONTESTED REGION

The Middlelands

A broad central war belt where the advance has stalled. The territory is too important to abandon, too costly to fully take and too unstable for ordinary life to recover normally.

FRONT LINE CELLSFixed positions, trenches, strongpoints and active exchanges.
SUPPORT LINE CELLSMedical posts, ammunition, repair, command and reinforcement.
REAR LINE CELLSSupply, rest, civilian services and protected infrastructure.
BREACH POINTSPlaces where an offensive nearly succeeded or may reopen.
DEAD GROUNDExposed crossings, abandoned fields and areas neither side can safely hold.
CIVILIAN HOLDOUTSCommunities that remain despite the battle line around them.
SALVAGE ZONESWrecks, abandoned stores, damaged infrastructure and risky recovery routes.
SUPPLY CORRIDORSRail, roads and service routes whose control can matter more than open territory.
