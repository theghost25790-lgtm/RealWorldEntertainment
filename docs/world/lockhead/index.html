Peace through strength

Weapons, supply and military force are treated as stabilising systems: power exists so that threats can be answered before they spread.

Industrial role

Lockhead is Project 2088's weapons-manufacturing analogue: an arms producer whose products and influence survive into the corporate order of Britana.
