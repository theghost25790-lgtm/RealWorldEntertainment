LONDON PREFECTURE

The largest Hive in Britana: immense, bureaucratic and certain that authority naturally flows outward from London.

RWE-P2088-LOC-LONDONPUBLISHED

The largest Hive

The City of London Hive is a jurisdiction and prefecture in its own right. It behaves as though it governs the other Hives whether or not those Hives accept the claim.

Character

London is designed to feel clean, immense, bureaucratic and politically confident. Its hierarchy is not presented internally as ambition; it is treated as the natural order of things.

Archive line

London does not call itself the capital. It calls the others provinces.
