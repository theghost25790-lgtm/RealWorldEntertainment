NEO VICTORIA

Nottingham below Nottingham

Neo Victoria is Britana's second-largest Hive. It expanded beneath and through Nottingham using abandoned coal pits, cave systems, transport tunnels and old civil infrastructure.

Character

Compared with London, Neo Victoria is more improvised, industrial and visibly layered. Old Britain remains physically legible beneath the corporate city built through it.

Archive line

They did not rebuild Nottingham. They taught the ground to hold another city.
