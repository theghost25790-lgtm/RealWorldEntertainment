# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Project 2088 free?

The intended player-facing model is free access, supported by selective sponsorship and advertising integrated into the world.

## How is it installed?

Current builds are standalone APKs downloaded to a PC and installed onto a compatible Quest headset using SideQuest.

## Can I play an older build?

Recoverable APKs will be preserved through GitHub Releases. Builds sharing the same package identifier may replace one another when installed.

## Is the Unreal Engine source public?

No. This repository is the public distribution and documentation hub, not the editable development project.

## Are advertisements forced over gameplay?

The design principle is environmental integration: advertisements should exist on suitable surfaces and within the world rather than interrupting play with forced overlays.
